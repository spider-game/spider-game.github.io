About the Game

A spider wanders through a maze, encountering obstacles in search of the answer to "What am I?". Enter commands to decide where to go and what to do, then confirm and see the result. This puzzle adventure helps improve logical thinking (not medical advice—I'm not a doctor!).

  • Pixel art style with forest mazes.
  • Command-based puzzle-platforming.
  • Destructible levels (coming soon).
  • Quick sessions: A few seconds to minutes.
  • Made with Unity, in development.
